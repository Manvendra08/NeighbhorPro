High-Impact Feature Ideas
🔥 Tier 1 — Maximum Adoption Impact
Group Booking / Pool Sessions
Residents collectively book one pro for a shared session — e.g., 8 households book yoga at ₹150/head vs ₹800 solo. Pro fills calendar. Residents save 80%. One feature, two-sided delight. Needs: groupSessions collection, min/max headcount, auto-confirm when threshold met.
Availability Slots + Instant Booking
Right now it's request-based. Pros should publish real time slots (like Calendly). Residents pick a slot and it's confirmed instantly. Removes the back-and-forth that kills conversion. Needs: availability subcollection per user.
Recurring Bookings
Weekly yoga, monthly CA review, fortnightly dietitian check-in. One-click "Subscribe to this" → auto-books every N days. Killer for retention on both sides.
Video Consultation (Built-in)
Integrate Daily.co or 100ms (both have free tiers). CA, legal, mental health, career coaching — none of these need physical presence. Adds a whole segment of professionals who otherwise won't join because they don't live in the society.

🏘️ Tier 2 — Society Ecosystem Features
Society Noticeboard
Replaces the chaos of 300-member WhatsApp groups. Structured posts: Lost & Found | For Sale | Need Help | Recommendations | Alerts. Admin-moderated. Residents actually want this badly.
Community Events Calendar
Society AGM, Diwali party, kids' activity class, club meetings. Any resident or society admin can post. Pros can list recurring classes here too (Wednesday 7am Yoga at Clubhouse → direct booking).
Resident Marketplace
Buy/sell within the society — trusted unlike OLX because identity is verified through the app. Second-hand furniture, kids' toys, appliances. No blue-collar services, just resident-to-resident exchange.
Amenity Booking
Clubhouse, swimming pool slots, badminton court, party lawn — residents book through the app instead of calling the guard. Society admin controls availability. Massive daily-use driver.

🧠 Tier 3 — Trust & Engagement
Neighbor Endorsements
Beyond star ratings: "Endorsed by 4 neighbors in Tower B." Socially powerful in a closed community. Far more trusted than anonymous internet reviews.
Verified Pro Badges
🎓 Degree Verified · 🪪 ID Verified · ✅ Background Acknowledged. Admin can toggle these after document check. Critical for health, legal, mental health categories.
Society Exclusives / Flash Deals
Pros offer time-limited deals only visible to specific society residents. "₹499 tax filing for Prestige residents, valid this week." Creates urgency, drives first bookings.
Skill Exchange / Barter Credits
CA gives tax advice → earns 2 NeighborCredits → redeems for yoga sessions. Non-monetary exchange for residents who want to contribute skills without formal pricing. Builds community feel.
Emergency / On-Demand
Pros toggle "Available Now" status. Residents post urgent requests with a premium rate. Doctor consultation, urgent legal matter, last-minute event planner. Uber-style availability ping.

📱 Tier 4 — Stickiness & Growth
Wellness Challenges — Society-wide 30-day fitness/meditation challenges run by a pro. Leaderboard, check-ins, group accountability. Creates daily app opens.
Document Vault — Residents securely share docs with booked pros (tax papers to CA, lab reports to doctor). Encrypted, permission-based. Huge trust differentiator vs WhatsApp sharing.
Kids & Family Hub — Filter by age group, subject, activity type (dance/art/coding for kids). Parents are the biggest power users in any gated society.
Referral Credits — Resident refers another resident → both get ₹100 credit. Viral loop that costs you only on successful activation.
Multi-language — Hindi UI option. Opens Tier 2 cities (Pune, Hyderabad, Jaipur) where societies are booming but English-only apps lose.
