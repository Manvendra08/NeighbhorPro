## Getting Started

### Creating Your Account

1. **Visit the Website**: Navigate to the ProNeighbor platform URL
2. **Click "Sign Up"**: Select the registration option on the login page
3. **Fill in Details**:
   - Email address (must be valid)
   - Password (minimum 8 characters, mixed case with numbers)
   - Full name
   - Phone number (optional, Indian format +91)
4. **Enter Referral Code** (optional): If a friend referred you, enter their code (format: `PN` + 6 alphanumeric characters)
5. **Verify Email**: Check your inbox for a verification email and click the link
6. **Complete Your Profile**: Add a photo, bio, and residency details

### Signing In

- **Email/Password**: Enter your registered email and password
- **Google Sign-In**: Click "Sign in with Google" for one-click access
- **Phone OTP**: Enter your Indian mobile number and verify via SMS OTP

### Residency Verification

To build trust within the community:

1. Go to **My Account** → **Profile**
2. Scroll to **Residency Verification**
3. Upload proof (PDF or image, max 10MB):
   - Society ID card
   - Utility bill with address
   - Rental agreement
4. Wait for admin review (typically 24-48 hours)
5. Once verified, you'll see a ✓ badge on your profile

## For Residents

### Browsing Professionals

**Navigate to**: Dashboard → Browse Professionals

#### Search & Filter Options

| Filter | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Category** | Select service type (Tutoring, Yoga, Plumbing, etc.) |
| **Locality** | Filter by your society or nearby areas |
| **Rating** | Show top-rated professionals (4+ stars) |
| **Verified** | Only show residency-verified professionals |
| **Price Range** | Filter by hourly rate or free consultation |

#### Professional Profile

When viewing a professional's profile, you'll see:

- **Photo & Name**: Profile picture and display name
- **Verification Badge**: ✓ if residency-verified
- **Rating**: Average star rating (1-5) with review count
- **Bio**: Professional's description and experience
- **Skills**: List of services offered
- **Hourly Rate**: Pricing in NeighbourCoins (NC)
- **Availability Calendar**: Days and times available
- **Recent Reviews**: What other residents say
- **Contact Button**: Opens chat (after booking)
- **Book Now Button**: Starts the booking process

### Booking a Service

#### Step 1: Select Service & Time

1. Click **Book Now** on the professional's profile
2. Review the service details (description, duration, price)
3. Select a date from the availability calendar
4. Choose an available time slot
5. Add any notes for the professional (optional, max 500 chars)
6. Upload supporting documents if needed (max 20MB)

#### Step 2: Payment Confirmation

1. Review the cost breakdown:
   - **Service Price**: Professional's hourly rate
   - **Platform Fee**: 15% service charge
   - **Total**: Amount in NeighbourCoins (NC)
2. Check your wallet balance
3. If insufficient, click **Top Up** to buy more coins
4. Confirm the booking

#### Step 3: Booking Created

- Your booking is now in **"Pending"** status
- Coins are held in escrow (not yet transferred)
- The professional receives a notification
- You can view the booking in **My Bookings**

### Managing Your Bookings

**Navigate to**: Dashboard → My Bookings

#### Booking Statuses

| Status | Meaning | Actions Available |
|--------|---------|-------------------|
| **Pending** | Awaiting professional's response | Cancel |
| **Confirmed** | Professional accepted | Cancel, Chat |
| **Completed** | Service delivered | Write Review |
| **Reviewed** | You submitted a review | View Details |
| **Cancelled** | Booking was cancelled | View Details |

#### Cancelling a Booking

- You can cancel from **Pending** or **Confirmed** status
- Coins held in escrow are automatically refunded
- The professional is notified of the cancellation
- Optionally add a cancellation reason

#### Chatting with Professionals

After booking, you can message the professional:

1. Go to **Messages** in the sidebar
2. Select the conversation with the professional
3. Send text messages or attachments (images, PDFs)
4. Messages are delivered in real-time
5. Read receipts show when messages are seen

### Writing Reviews

After a booking is completed:

1. Go to **My Bookings** and find the completed booking
2. Click **Write Review**
3. Rate the service (1-5 stars)
4. Write your feedback (max 1000 characters)
5. Submit the review
6. **Earn 20 NC** for your review!

Your review helps other residents make informed decisions.

## For Professionals

### Setting Up Your Professional Profile

1. **Go to My Account** → **Profile**
2. **Enable Professional Mode**: Toggle "I offer services"
3. **Add Your Skills**: List services you provide
4. **Set Your Rate**: Hourly price in NC (or mark as "Free Consultation")
5. **Write Your Bio**: Describe your experience and expertise
6. **Upload a Photo**: Professional-looking profile picture
7. **Set Availability**: Define which days/times you're available

### Creating Service Listings

**Navigate to**: Profile → Manage Services

#### Creating a New Listing

1. Click **Add New Service**
2. Fill in:
   - **Title**: Clear, descriptive name (e.g., "Math Tutoring for Class 10-12")
   - **Category**: Select from available categories
   - **Description**: Detailed service description
   - **Duration**: How long each session takes
   - **Price**: Cost in NC (or mark as free)
3. Submit for review

**Note**: New listings are reviewed by admins before going live (typically 24 hours).

#### Business Categories

Some categories require an active subscription:

| Group | Categories |
|-------|------------|
| **Business** | Tuition & Coaching, Yoga & Fitness, Music & Dance, Language Classes, Nutrition & Diet |
| **Services** | Tax & CA, Legal Advisory, Accounting, Investment Planning, etc. |
| **E-Commerce** | Food & Catering, Apparels, Jewellery, Home Decor, etc. |

Business category listings are hidden if your subscription expires.

### Managing Bookings

**Navigate to**: Dashboard → My Bookings

#### Accepting Bookings

1. Check the **Pending** tab for new requests
2. Review the booking details (date, time, service, notes)
3. Click **Accept** to confirm or **Decline** to reject
4. Accepted bookings move to **Confirmed** status
5. The client is notified of your response

#### Completing Bookings

1. After delivering the service, go to **Confirmed** bookings
2. Click **Mark as Complete**
3. The escrowed coins are released to your wallet (minus 15% platform fee)
4. The client is prompted to write a review

#### Your Earnings

When a booking is completed:
- **Total Escrow**: Coins held by the client
- **Platform Fee**: 15% goes to ProNeighbor
- **Your Earnings**: 85% credited to your cashable balance

Example:
```
Client pays: 1000 NC (held in escrow)
Platform fee: 150 NC (15%)
Your earnings: 850 NC → Added to your cashable balance
```

### Setting Your Availability

**Navigate to**: Profile → Availability

Use the availability editor to:
- Mark days you're available (Monday through Sunday)
- Set specific time slots for each day
- Block out holidays or personal time
- Update availability in real-time

Your availability is shown to clients when they browse your profile.

### Withdrawing Earnings

**Navigate to**: Wallet → Cash Out tab

#### Requirements

- Minimum withdrawal: **200 NC**
- Only **cashable balance** can be withdrawn (not promo coins)
- Valid UPI ID required

#### Steps

1. Enter the amount to withdraw (minimum 200 NC)
2. Enter your UPI ID (e.g., `yourname@upi`)
3. Review the masked UPI display for privacy
4. Confirm the payout request
5. Wait for admin processing (typically 2-3 business days)
6. Track status in **Payout History**

#### Payout Status

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Pending** | Request submitted, awaiting admin review |
| **Processed** | Money transferred to your UPI |
| **Failed** | Transfer failed (contact support) |
| **Cancelled** | You cancelled the request (coins refunded) |

## Wallet & NeighbourCoins

### Understanding Your Wallet

**Navigate to**: Wallet

Your wallet has three balance types:

| Balance | Source | Can Withdraw? | Can Use For? |
|---------|--------|---------------|--------------|
| **💳 Cashable NC** | Top-ups, booking earnings, refunds | ✅ Yes | Bookings, subscriptions |
| **🎁 Promo NC** | Signup bonus, referrals, reviews | ❌ No | Bookings only |
| **Total NC** | Sum of both | — | Display purposes |

### Buying Coins (Top-Up)

**Navigate to**: Wallet → Buy tab

#### Available Packs

| Pack | Price (₹) | Base Coins | Bonus | Total NC |
|------|-----------|------------|-------|----------|
| Trial | ₹300 | 300 | 0 | 300 |
| Starter | ₹1,000 | 1,000 | 100 | 1,100 |
| **Popular** | ₹1,500 | 1,500 | 200 | **1,700** |
| Pro | ₹2,000 | 2,000 | 250 | 2,250 |
| Society | ₹2,500 | 2,500 | 500 | 3,000 |

#### Purchase Flow

1. Select a coin pack
2. Click **Buy Now**
3. Razorpay payment gateway opens
4. Complete payment (UPI, card, net banking)
5. Coins are instantly credited to your wallet
6. All purchased coins go to **cashable balance**

### Earning Coins

| Activity | Reward | Type |
|----------|--------|------|
| **Welcome Bonus** | 500 NC | Promo (one-time) |
| **Profile Completion** | 50 NC | Promo (one-time) |
| **Write a Review** | 20 NC | Promo (per review) |
| **Refer a Friend** | 200 NC | Promo (when they sign up) |
| **Friend's First Booking** | 200 NC | Promo (when they complete) |
| **Free Consultation Given** | 100 NC | Promo (per booking) |
| **Group Session Attended** | 25 NC | Promo |
| **On-Demand Request** | 50 NC | Promo |
| **Community Milestone** | 50 NC | Promo |

### Referral System

**Navigate to**: Wallet → Referral tab

#### How It Works

1. **Share Your Code**: Copy or share via WhatsApp
   - Format: `PN` + 6 characters (e.g., `PNABC123`)
2. **Friend Signs Up**: They enter your code during registration
3. **You Earn 200 NC**: Credited immediately when they sign up
4. **Friend Earns 200 NC**: When they complete their first booking
5. **Both Win**: Total 400 NC distributed!

#### Tracking Referrals

View your referral history:
- Friends who signed up with your code
- Status of each referral (pending/rewarded)
- Total NC earned from referrals

### Transaction History

**Navigate to**: Wallet → History tab

View all coin transactions:
- **Date & Time**: When the transaction occurred
- **Type**: Transaction category (topup, booking, earning, etc.)
- **Amount**: Positive (credit) or negative (debit)
- **Balance After**: Your balance after this transaction
- **Description**: Human-readable explanation

#### Transaction Types

| Type | Description |
|------|-------------|
| `topup` | Coin purchase via Razorpay |
| `booking_escrow` | Coins held for a booking |
| `booking_escrow_release` | Earnings from completed booking |
| `booking_refund` | Refund from cancelled booking |
| `earn_*` | Various earning mechanisms |
| `payout` | Withdrawal to bank account |
| `subscription_debit` | Subscription payment |

## Subscriptions

### Understanding Business Subscriptions

Business-category professionals need an active subscription to keep listings visible.

### Available Plans

| Plan | Duration | Price (NC) | Monthly Rate | Savings |
|------|----------|------------|--------------|---------|
| **Free Trial** | 30 days | 0 | Free | — |
| 3 Months | 90 days | 999 | 333/mo | — |
| **6 Months** | 180 days | 1,799 | 300/mo | ✨ Best Value |
| 12 Months | 365 days | 2,299 | 192/mo | 42% off |

### Starting a Free Trial

1. Go to **Subscription** in the sidebar
2. Click **Start Free Trial**
3. Your trial activates immediately
4. Business category listings become visible
5. 30-day trial period begins

**Important**: Only one trial per account, ever.

### Purchasing a Subscription

1. Go to **Subscription** in the sidebar
2. Select a plan (3/6/12 months)
3. Verify you have enough **cashable NC**
4. Click **Subscribe**
5. Coins are debited from your cashable balance
6. Subscription activates immediately
7. Any paused listings are restored

### Managing Your Subscription

**Navigate to**: Subscription

#### View Current Status

- **Plan Name**: Your active plan
- **Expires On**: When your subscription ends
- **Days Remaining**: Countdown to expiry
- **Auto-Renew**: Whether renewal is scheduled

#### Actions Available

| Action | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Renew** | Purchase a new plan before expiry |
| **Cancel** | Stop auto-renewal (access until period end) |
| **Resume** | Reactivate a cancelled subscription |
| **View Invoices** | Download past payment receipts |

### Renewal Reminders

You'll receive notifications at:
- **T-7 days**: "Subscription renews in 7 days"
- **T-3 days**: "Subscription renews in 3 days"
- **T-1 day**: "Subscription renews tomorrow"

### What Happens on Expiry

1. **Grace Period**: 5 days of continued access
2. **Listing Pause**: Business category listings hidden
3. **Notification**: "Business listing paused - renew to reactivate"
4. **Manual Renewal**: Purchase a new plan to restore listings

## PWA Installation

### What is a PWA?

A Progressive Web App (PWA) provides a native app experience:
- Install on your home screen
- Works offline (cached content)
- Push notifications
- Fast loading

### Installing on Mobile

#### Android (Chrome)

1. Open ProNeighbor in Chrome
2. Tap the menu (⋮) → **Add to Home screen**
3. Or tap the install banner when it appears
4. Confirm installation
5. App icon appears on home screen

#### iOS (Safari)

1. Open ProNeighbor in Safari
2. Tap the Share button (□ with arrow)
3. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**
4. Confirm the name and tap **Add**
5. App icon appears on home screen

### Installing on Desktop

#### Chrome/Edge

1. Look for the install icon (⊕) in the address bar
2. Click **Install**
3. Or use the browser menu → **Install ProNeighbor**
4. App opens in its own window

### Using the Installed App

- Launch from home screen or app drawer
- Sign in if prompted (session persists)
- Full-screen experience without browser UI
- Receive push notifications
- Works with limited connectivity

### Enabling Push Notifications

1. When prompted, allow notifications
2. Or go to browser settings → Notifications → Allow for ProNeighbor
3. You'll receive alerts for:
   - New booking requests
   - Booking status changes
   - Messages
   - Subscription reminders

## FAQ & Troubleshooting

### Account Issues

**Q: I didn't receive the verification email**
- Check your spam/junk folder
- Verify you entered the correct email
- Request a new verification email from the login page

**Q: I forgot my password**
- Click "Forgot Password" on the login page
- Enter your registered email
- Follow the reset link in your inbox

**Q: My account is disabled**
- Contact support via the Support page
- An admin will review your case

### Booking Issues

**Q: My booking is stuck in "Pending"**
- The professional may be offline
- Wait 24-48 hours, then contact support
- You can cancel and book another professional

**Q: I can't cancel my booking**
- Cancellations are only allowed before the service starts
- Contact support if you need to cancel after confirmation

**Q: The professional didn't show up**
- Contact them via the in-app chat
- If no response, raise a dispute via Support
- Admin will review and process a refund if warranted

### Wallet Issues

**Q: My coins didn't arrive after payment**
- Wait 5 minutes (processing delay)
- Check your transaction history
- If still missing, contact support with payment ID

**Q: Why can't I withdraw my promo coins?**
- Promo coins (from bonuses, referrals, reviews) are non-withdrawable
- Only cashable coins (from top-ups, earnings, refunds) can be withdrawn
- This prevents money laundering and bonus exploitation

**Q: My payout is taking too long**
- Payouts are processed within 2-3 business days
- Check your payout status in Wallet → Cash Out
- Contact support if pending for more than 5 days

### Subscription Issues

**Q: My business listings are hidden**
- Check if your subscription has expired
- Go to Subscription page to renew
- Listings restore immediately after renewal

**Q: Can I get a refund on my subscription?**
- Subscriptions are non-refundable once activated
- You can cancel to prevent renewal
- Contact support for exceptional cases

**Q: How do I change my subscription plan?**
- Cancel your current subscription
- Wait for it to expire (or contact support)
- Purchase the new plan

### Technical Issues

**Q: The app is slow or not loading**
- Clear browser cache and cookies
- Try a different browser
- Check your internet connection
- Disable browser extensions

**Q: Push notifications aren't working**
- Check browser notification permissions
- Ensure the app is installed (for mobile)
- Try re-enabling notifications in settings

**Q: I can't upload files**
- Check file size limits (profile: 5MB, proofs: 10MB, attachments: 20MB)
- Ensure file format is supported (images, PDFs)
- Try a different browser

### Contact Support

**Navigate to**: Support

- Browse FAQs
- Create a support ticket
- Track ticket status
- Receive email notifications

For urgent issues, contact the platform admin directly.
