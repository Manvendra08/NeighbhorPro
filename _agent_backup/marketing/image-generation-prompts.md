# ProNeighbor — Image Generation Prompts
## Marketing Assets Pack v1.0

**Brand DNA:**
- Primary: Teal (#0D6B6B) — trust, calm, professional
- Accent: Terracotta (#D45C3B) — warmth, community, India
- Background: Warm off-white (#FAF8F3) — premium, clean
- Typography feel: Playfair Display (editorial serif) + DM Sans (modern sans)
- Aesthetic: Premium glassmorphism + warm Indian gated-community setting
- Tone: Aspirational but grounded — not startup-generic, not corporate-cold

**Usage rules for all prompts:**
- No real people's faces (use silhouettes, hands, or shot from behind)
- No logos of competitors
- Text in image: avoid unless specified — add via Canva overlay
- All should feel like they belong to the same premium Indian urban lifestyle brand

### 01. Instagram Feed — Hero / Brand Awareness
**Platform:** Instagram Feed (1:1, 1080×1080px)
**Purpose:** Brand introduction — "What is ProNeighbor"

```

at golden hour. Lush gardens, premium apartment towers in warm cream and terracotta,

Warm cinematic color grade — golden amber and soft shadows. Photorealistic, shot with
85mm lens, f/1.8 bokeh. Aspirational Indian urban real estate aesthetic. No people.
```
